Privacy Policy — Kairo

Last updated: 18-10-2026

This policy explains what data Kairo ("the app", "we", "us") collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. Kairo is a time-tracking app with an optional AI Coach feature.

1. Data stored on your device

Kairo stores the following directly on your device, and does not send it anywhere unless you use the AI Coach or Export features described below:

  • Categories you create (names, colors, icons)
  • Time entries you track (start/end times, durations, which category)
  • Goals and habits you set, and your progress toward them
  • App preferences (theme, language, notification settings)

This data stays on your device. If you uninstall the app, it is deleted, except for whatever your phone's own operating system backup feature (e.g. Android's "Back up my data") may have already copied to your own personal cloud storage account — that backup is controlled by your device's OS settings, not by Kairo directly.

2. AI Coach (Premium feature)

If you use the AI Coach — an optional, subscriber-only feature — the question or request you type, along with a summary of your tracked time relevant to answering it (category names and durations, goal and habit progress; never anything else on your device), is sent to Anthropic (the maker of the Claude AI model that powers this feature) to generate a response. See Anthropic's own privacy policy for how they handle this data: https://www.anthropic.com/privacy.

We do not store your AI Coach conversations on our servers beyond what's needed to generate each response in the moment, with one exception: reported replies.

Reporting an AI Coach reply

Under every AI Coach reply there is a "Report" button. If you use it, we store on our server: the reply you reported, the question you asked right before it, the reason you chose, the time of the report, and the anonymous device identifier described in Section 3. We use this only to review problematic AI output and improve the AI Coach's safety and accuracy. Reports are never shared with third parties and are deleted after at most 12 months. Nothing is stored unless you choose to report a reply.

3. Account identification

To determine whether you have an active Premium subscription and to apply fair usage limits to the AI Coach, the app generates a random, anonymous identifier stored on your device and sends it to our server along with your Google Play purchase information. This identifier is not linked to your name, email address, or any other personally identifying information we control — it exists solely to distinguish one device's subscription and usage from another's.

4. Google Play Billing

Subscription purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made (via Google's Play Developer API) so we can unlock Premium features, but we do not receive or store your payment card details — those are handled exclusively by Google. See Google Play's own privacy policy for how they handle payment information: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Data you export

Kairo lets you export your own data (as CSV, JSON, PDF, or a calendar file) directly to your device, for you to save, share, or back up as you choose. This export happens locally on your device — we do not receive a copy of it, and this is a Premium feature.

6. Data we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, or any device identifiers beyond the anonymous, purchase-linked identifier described in Section 3. We do not sell your data to third parties, and we do not show advertising in Kairo.

7. Children's privacy

Kairo is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Your choices

  • You can disable the AI Coach's automatic weekly summary and streak celebrations at any time in Profile > Notifications.
  • You can export or delete your own data at any time from within the app (Profile > Export data; uninstalling the app removes all locally-stored data).
  • You can revoke notification and background-activity permissions at any time through your device's system settings.
  • You can ask us to delete any AI Coach reply you reported by emailing the address in Section 10 with roughly when you reported it and what it was about.

Delete your Kairo data

The Kairo app has no user accounts. Your data is deleted as follows:

  • Data on your phone (categories, time entries, goals, habits, settings): delete it yourself at any time by uninstalling Kairo, or via Android Settings > Apps > Kairo > Storage > Clear data. Nothing of this is stored on our server.
  • Data on our server: send an email to kairo.ai.timetracker@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Kairo data". Mention roughly when you used the AI Coach or reported a reply, so we can find your records. We then delete, within 30 days: the anonymous device identifier linked to your installation, its Premium status and AI usage counter, and any AI Coach replies you reported.
  • What is kept: Google keeps its own record of your Google Play purchases under Google's privacy policy; we cannot delete that. Nothing else is retained by us after your request.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to: kairo.ai.timetracker@gmail.com
